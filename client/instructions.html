YOUR STARTING POINT

Save the way it should look.

In your library, choose New setup. Give it a name, then choose Upload photo or Use camera to add a tidy reference. Choose Save setup.

Already have a setup? Open its card and go straight to step 02. The example setups are editable, too.

REFERENCE
02

ADDING A PHOTO FOR AI

Add how it looks right now.

  1. Open the setup you want to check in Setup library.
  2. In Visual comparison, choose Add current or Add current photo. If a current photo is already saved, choose Replace current.
  3. Choose Upload a photo to pick an image, or Use camera to take one. For camera capture, choose Take photo, review it, then choose Use this photo.
  4. Your current photo saves automatically. You can drag the slider to compare it with the reference.
Adding it from the AI window?

Choose Add current photo there, upload or take a photo, then choose AI comparison again.

Use a similar angle, distance and lighting. Keep the same area in both photos. Use Add current for a fresh check; Replace reference changes your baseline and clears the old current photo.

CURRENT
03

A SECOND PAIR OF EYES

Ask AI what changed.

  1. Choose AI comparison below the photo viewer.
  2. If asked, sign in to your SnapBack email account. New accounts need email confirmation. Return to the same browser to keep using your saved library.
  3. Check that the REFERENCE and CURRENT previews show the two photos you want to compare.
  4. Read the sharing notice and tick the agreement if it applies to you. The current AI feature is for people aged 18 or older using it for work.
  5. Choose Compare with Groq ✦ and wait for the report.

AI is free with sign-in and usage limits. Only these two resized photos are sent to Groq after you agree. Uploading or taking a photo does not start AI automatically.

FREE AI
04

BACK TO JUST RIGHT

Review, then make it a checklist.

Check the suggested changes against the photos. For a useful suggestion, choose Add review step to checklist. You can edit the text and tick each item as you reset the space.

AI can miss details or mistake camera movement for a change. Review every suggestion yourself. It is not a complete inventory or a safety inspection.

RESET

A LITTLE HELP

Stuck on a step?

Most comparisons just need the right two photos.

Why is Compare with Groq greyed out?

Add a current photo, sign in, and tick the sharing agreement after reviewing it. Both photo previews must be present. If availability is still loading, choose Refresh AI availability. Shared service limits can temporarily pause AI.

I can’t find Add current.

Return to Setup library and open a setup card. Look in the Visual comparison panel below the cards; on a phone, scroll down to it. The button changes to Replace current when a photo is already saved. You can also choose Add current photo inside the AI window.

The camera won’t open.

Allow camera access for SnapBack in your browser. If permission is blocked or there is no camera, choose Upload a photo instead. Camera capture works on the public HTTPS app and on localhost. No audio is recorded.

My photo won’t upload.

Use a JPG, PNG or WebP image up to 20 MB. HEIC and HEIF work only when your browser can decode them; exporting the photo as JPG usually helps. Wait for the saved message before opening AI comparison.

Can I compare without signing in?

Yes. Upload both photos and use the slider or Side by side view. A verified SnapBack account is needed for AI. There is no subscription or checkout.

Where are my photos saved?

Your reference photos, current photos and checklists stay in this browser on this device. They do not sync between accounts or devices. Use Backup & storage to export your library before clearing browser data.

Why did AI ask me to wait?

Allow at least one minute between AI attempts. SnapBack also has a shared limit of 25 attempts per day and a limit of 100 attempts per person each month. Provider limits can pause comparisons earlier. You can keep comparing manually.
